Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information 556678.com ("we", "us") collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (growth plan, contact, newsletter, contest entry, job application, contribution or partnership inquiry) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Forms are delivered to us using the third-party form service FormSubmit. We use this information only to respond to you and provide what you asked for. We do not sell your personal information.

Calculator inputs

Numbers you enter into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not transmitted to us.

Cookies and local storage

We use local storage to remember preferences such as dark mode and your cookie choice. Third parties described below may set cookies.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent message managed through Google's consent tools, where required.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may collect data under Google's privacy policy. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing. Contact us through our contact page to make a request.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect their information.

Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate record-keeping, and use reasonable safeguards to protect them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
