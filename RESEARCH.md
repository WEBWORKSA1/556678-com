| Generic AI tools directory | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 13 |

**Decision:** "556678 — Count In Your Growth." Free business calculators + plain-English playbooks + a free growth/funding plan funnel. Finance/loans/business-software are among the highest-CPC AdSense verticals, calculators drive repeat visits and links, and the growth-plan funnel produces sellable B2B leads (lenders, accountants, SaaS).
