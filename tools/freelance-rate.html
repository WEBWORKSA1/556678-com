Billable hours are fewer than you think

A 40-hour week rarely means 40 billable hours. Sales calls, admin, invoicing, learning and unpaid revisions eat into the week. Many independent professionals bill 20–30 hours in a typical week, and vacation, holidays and sick days remove several weeks a year.

This calculator starts with what you want to take home, adds business expenses, grosses up for tax and divides by realistic billable hours. The result is your minimum viable rate — not your market rate.

Move from hours to outcomes

Once you know your floor, package your work: fixed-price projects, retainers and productised services let you capture the value you create rather than the time you spend.

FAQ

Should I publish my hourly rate?

Many consultants publish project starting prices instead, which anchors on value and avoids hourly comparisons.

How often should I raise rates?

Review at least yearly and whenever your calendar is consistently full.
