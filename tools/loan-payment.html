Reading a loan offer like a lender

The monthly payment is only the start. Look at the total interest — the real price of the money — and the annual debt service, which lenders compare with your yearly operating cash flow. Most conventional lenders want to see cash flow of at least 1.25 times annual debt service before they are comfortable.

Watch out for

  • Factor rates quoted on short-term or merchant cash advances. A 1.3 factor on a 6-month advance can equal an APR well above 50%.
  • Origination and packaging fees that are deducted from the amount you receive.
  • Prepayment penalties that remove the benefit of paying early.

Always compare offers on APR and total cost, not on the monthly payment alone.

FAQ

Is this the exact payment I'll get?

It is an estimate based on a standard amortizing loan. Your lender's fees, rate type and payment schedule may differ.

What is a good APR for a small business loan?

It varies widely with credit, collateral, time in business and lender type. Government-backed programs and banks are usually cheaper than online short-term lenders.
