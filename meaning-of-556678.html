The story behind the name

Why "556678"? The count-in behind the brand

Read it aloud — "fifty-five, sixty-six, seventy-eight" — and it climbs. Break it apart and it becomes the most famous count-in in performance: five, six, seven, eight.

The dancer's count-in

Choreography is usually built in phrases of eight beats. Instead of counting a whole phrase aloud, instructors call the last four beats — "five, six, seven, eight!" — to set the tempo and get everyone starting on the same downbeat. It's quick, it's rhythmic, and it means "get ready: we go now." That is exactly the moment this website is built for: the instant before a founder launches, prices, hires or borrows.

A rising sequence

The digits never go down: 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 8. Two steady steps, then acceleration. It's a neat picture of healthy growth — consolidate, consolidate, then climb — and it inspired our logo of four rising bars.

Numbers and commerce across cultures

Numbers carry meaning in business culture around the world. In Chinese numerology, 6 and 8 are widely considered lucky: 8 is associated with prosperity and 6 with things going smoothly, which is why addresses, phone numbers and licence plates rich in these digits can command premiums. In Western numerology, 5 is often linked with change and trade, and 7 with good fortune. We don't make claims about luck — we prefer calculators — but we like that the number reads well in many cultures.
