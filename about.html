About 556678.com

We build free, practical tools that help people start and grow businesses with confidence.

556678.com started from a simple observation: most small-business failures are not caused by bad ideas but by controllable problems — running out of cash, under-pricing, not knowing the break-even point, or growing faster than the numbers allow. The information to avoid those mistakes exists, but it is often buried in jargon or behind paywalls.

What we do

  • Free calculators for cash runway, break-even, pricing, margins, loans, growth, unit economics and AI return on investment.
  • Original guides written in plain English and reviewed for accuracy against public sources.
  • A curated video library of talks from experienced builders.
  • Free growth plans reviewed by a person, with optional introductions to vetted partners.
  • The 8-Count Challenge, a monthly contest that rewards founders for taking action.

Editorial standards

We write for owners, not accountants. We cite public data sources, label sponsored content clearly, and keep advertising separate from editorial decisions. If you spot an error, tell us and we'll fix it.

How we're funded

Advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, disclosed partner referrals and contributions from supporters. This keeps every tool free.
